## 2. The problem: a decision outlives its evidence

Documents, entity records, chain state and policy versions change independently. A reviewer needs to know what was observed, when, under which policy, and what another human decided. The product addresses traceability, not automated legal certification. Visual: four evidence inputs converging on one review.

## 3. One connected workflow

Create case ? private upload ? cited extraction ? external snapshots ? deterministic checks ? human review ? immutable report ? independent verification. Visual: the implemented architecture diagram; no unsupported performance numbers.
